Welcome to Nsanje's Voice

Nyanthepa Radio 107.6 FM is a community-driven station broadcasting straight from the heart of Nsanje Boma. We bring you the latest local news, vibrant sports coverage, and the music that moves the Lower Shire Valley.

Our presenters are passionate locals who understand your world, your challenges, and your celebrations. Whether you're farming, trading, or commuting — we're with you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I listen to Nyanthepa FM?▾
Click the big play button on our homepage! You can also tune in on FM 107.6 if you're within our broadcast range in the Nsanje district.
How do I request a song?▾
Head to the "Request a Song" page using the button in the navigation bar. Fill in your name and the song details — our presenters will try to play it during the next show!
Can I download music from this site?▾
Yes! Visit the Music Archive page to find tracks available for free download. Look for the download button (⬇) next to each track.
How can I advertise with Nyanthepa FM?▾
We offer on-air advertising, website banners, and community sponsorships. Visit our Brand Partnership page or contact us directly for rates and packages.
